Garmin Manual: Conversion of timestamp_16 to timestamp

Most rows in a Garmin monitoring file hold only a short time number called timestamp_16. This walk-through shows, one click at a time, how two yellow columns in the workbook turn it into a real date and time. No Excel experience is needed.
